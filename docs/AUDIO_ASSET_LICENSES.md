# Audio asset licenses

The ten celebration sounds introduced in test build 196 originate from Mixkit and are used under the Mixkit Sound Effects Free License.

Source collection: https://mixkit.co/free-sound-effects/win/

License: https://mixkit.co/license/#sfxFree

Included Mixkit asset IDs: 2012, 2010, 2011, 523, 462, 610, 1934, 1996, 1928 and 226.

The source and asset IDs are retained here so their provenance can be audited later. They replace the synthetic movie-inspired voice options in the separate test build; no original movie dialogue or actor recordings are included.
